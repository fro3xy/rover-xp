Meet Rover!

The animated search companion from Windows XP. Your friendly helper since October 25, 2001.

About Rover

Rover was the default Search Companion in Windows XP: a cartoon dog in the left pane while you looked for files, folders, and other computers.

He shipped with XP on October 25, 2001, drawn on the Microsoft Agent technology. The character files lived under %windir%\srchasst.

His first desk job was older than XP. In Microsoft Bob, March 1995, he was Rover Retriever, the golden retriever who walked people through a virtual house. XP kept the dog and gave him a search box.

On screen he was patient, eager, and a little silly. He tilted his head, wagged, and never spoke English. The sounds were recorded barks and foley, so he felt more alive than a clip-art mascot.

You could swap him for Merlin the wizard, Links the cat, or Courtney. Most people left the dog where he was.

Fans still pass around unused character notes: an optimistic helper who knew he lived in software. XP never told that story. The dog you actually got just helped you search.

Windows Vista retired the animated Search Companion in 2007. The pane went quiet. Rover did not get a farewell tour.

The idea of a helper sitting beside your work showed up again in later assistants. None of them had his collar, or the Bliss hill behind the window.
